# SEFI-PY Evolution — Historical Research and Simulation Projects

This directory collects earlier computational explorations, educational demonstrations, and engineering-oriented research prototypes associated with GWFM, SEFI, and DEFI.

**Scientific status:** These projects do not constitute a validated unified physical theory, a complete four-dimensional field engine, or experimental evidence for proposed geometric interpretations. Read each project's methods, test records, and limitations before interpreting its results.

## Projects and documentation

- [Double Slit Explorer](sim/double_slit_explorer/README.md) — educational interference visualization using a standard quantum reference distribution; proposed interpretations are illustrative.
- [Vortex Research Console](sim/vortex_console/README.md) — numerical field profiles and restricted dynamical investigations; not a demonstrated stable physical particle.
- [Calibration workbench](research/calibration_workbench/README.md) — synthetic microwave-drift diagnosis and recovery study; not a deployed or company-endorsed system.
- [Physical matter checkpoint](research/physical_matter_2026_09_23/README.md) — finite-domain model investigations, including recorded unsuccessful searches and open stability questions.

## Research provenance

This directory preserves prior research phases. Some material predates later mathematical audits and should not be read as a current claim of physical validity or algorithmic advantage. Numerical consistency, software correctness, mathematical proof, and experimental validation remain distinct.
