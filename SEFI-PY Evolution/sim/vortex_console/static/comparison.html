Background wave comparison

Compare your rotating background with a static global-Witten control. This is an exact small-disturbance calculation in the homogeneous region outside a vortex.

What changes?

The static control has long-wavelength phase speed 1. The rotating background gives 1 / sqrt(5) = 0.4472: a 55.28% decrease, in units where light speed is 1.

Both use the same effective amplitude stiffness and dilute carrier mass. Their physical potentials and background charges differ. This is not an equal-charge isolated-vortex experiment.

What the equations say

omega_plus/minus^2 = k^2 + 1 + 2 Omega^2 +/- sqrt((1 + 2 Omega^2)^2 + 4 Omega^2 k^2)

The control uses Omega = 0; the rotating realization uses Omega = sqrt(2). These are mode frequencies relative to the selected background, calculated in its rest frame. They are independent of the moving-ring controls in the main console.

This does not change causal light speed. It does not establish vortex stability, a particle identification, propulsion, or experimental confirmation. Rotating condensates are also describable with conventional scalar-field theory.
