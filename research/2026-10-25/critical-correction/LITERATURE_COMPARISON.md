Working assessment, 25 October 2026. This is a targeted primary-source comparison, not a systematic or exhaustive novelty review.

## Normalization and model overlap

With Phi=sqrt(2) psi and the stationary Sigma amplitude s real, this study's canonical action has charged kinetic term |partial psi|^2, neutral amplitude kinetic term (partial s)^2/2, and potential

    V=|psi|^4+|psi|^2+4|psi|^2 s^2
         +(lambda/4)(s^2-3/lambda)^2.

The stationary amplitude sector is therefore an FLS-type model with charged-field self-interaction and a bare mass term. In the cited 2024 model the displayed basic potential is h^2|psi|^2 s^2+(m^2/2)(s^2-v^2)^2. The additional terms and the critical parameter regime must be compared explicitly with further self-interacting FLS literature.

Although the underlying Sigma field is complex here, its phase is held constant in the stationary calculation. That extra phase alone does not make the amplitude results novel.

## Candidate contribution to examine

A formal near-critical constrained-curve reduction retaining the induced kinetic metric, a first transverse-amplitude correction, on-shell charge coefficients at two distinct thresholds, and prospective numerical checks at new couplings.

The coefficient formulas follow standard perturbative/elimination and envelope reasoning. Their original application to this exact potential family has not been established by the present search.

## Further review needed

Search self-interacting FLS models, near-degenerate quartic potentials, stationary derivative expansions, nonlinear sigma-model reductions and singular limits for charged scalar solitons. Determine whether the same reduced functional or correction coefficients exist after parameter changes. If equivalent results exist, position this work as verification, extension or a reproducible benchmark rather than a new theory.

No claim of priority, first discovery or established novelty is approved.
