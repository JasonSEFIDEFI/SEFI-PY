# Single-Field Research Roadmap — six proof obligations

**Researcher:** Jason Duran Dutton  
**Program:** SEFI / GWFM / DEFI  
**Status:** Research questions and proof obligations, not proven SEFI results.  
**Date:** 2026-11-16

## Central research question

Can a minimally constrained mathematical structure produce geometry, distinguishable physical entities, and their interactions as emergent properties, rather than requiring those structures to be specified beforehand?

**Hypothesis to investigate, not a premise:** phenomena presently described by multiple physical fields may arise from one underlying mathematical field or entity. A single field can possess multiple independent components or degrees of freedom; a one-field claim must specify what is meant by “one.”

## Proof obligations

1. **Well-defined mathematics and consistency.** Explicitly specify primitive objects, state space, dynamics, regularity, symmetries, boundary/initial conditions, and observables. Establish existence, uniqueness where required, stability/continuation or precisely delimit failure modes. Do not invent a field equation to force a preferred result.
2. **Emergence of distinguishable entities.** Derive nontrivial persistent structures from the primitive dynamics without encoding the target particle identities into definitions. Specify objective criteria for persistence, identity, separation, interactions, and parameter dependence.
3. **Quantum correspondence.** Recover relevant quantum state/probability structure, interference, unitary or appropriate open-system evolution, measurement statistics, and quantitative benchmark predictions under explicit limiting assumptions. Do not equate visualization or analogy with derivation.
4. **Particle and interaction correspondence.** Account for observed spin/statistics, charges, masses, gauge structures, conservation laws, interactions, and empirical precision where claiming Standard Model coverage. Partial models must declare their restricted scope.
5. **Spacetime and gravitation.** If geometry is claimed emergent, derive causal/metric structure and gravitational dynamics, recovering tested general-relativistic regimes and specifying where deviations occur. This obligation may have independent prerequisites and need not be solved in the displayed order.
6. **Distinctive falsifiable predictions and experimental comparison.** Compute measurable departures or uniquely constrained outcomes with uncertainty, controls, and reproducible protocols. Physical confirmation is empirical, not a theorem.

## Evidence gates

- **Definition:** an explicitly chosen mathematical object or rule, not evidence of nature.
- **Assumption:** required premise, justified or openly unresolved.
- **Theorem:** stated with hypotheses and proof or exact independently checkable certificate.
- **Numerical evidence:** reproducible computation with error bounds where needed; never promoted to theorem automatically.
- **Physical correspondence:** a justified mapping from mathematical objects to operationally measurable quantities and recovery of benchmark results.
- **Empirical validation:** independent observation/experiment and comparison with alternatives.

Every downstream claim must stop when a necessary prior definition, derivation, correspondence, or validation is missing. Negative results and corrections remain recorded.

## Research dependency outline

Primitive structure and dynamics -> mathematical well-posedness -> emergent structures and operational observables -> quantitative quantum/particle and spacetime correspondences (potentially parallel, with further dependencies) -> discriminating predictions -> independent experimental assessment.

This is a research map, **not** a proof of SEFI or a claim that the dependencies are already discharged.

## First admissible milestone

Find a mathematically motivated, minimally constrained primitive dynamics and prove a nontrivial theorem about its persistent, distinguishable solutions without importing the target physical entities as axioms. Then investigate, separately, whether any such solution has quantitatively defensible physical correspondence. An illustrative equation such as ∂tΦ=F[Φ] is notation only, not an adopted SEFI evolution law.

## Scope guard

The repository's exact switched-matrix stability results are valid only for their specified mathematical families. Their connection to a physically defined single-field theory has **not** been derived. Do not infer physical unity, particle identity, quantum error correction performance, or consciousness from these results.
