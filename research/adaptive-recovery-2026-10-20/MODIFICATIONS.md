# Modifications and provenance

Author project: Jason Duran Dutton, SEFI Research Program.

This package adds a separate streaming-noise benchmark, conventional adaptive estimators, causal and numerical tests, frozen protocol, development selection, evaluation records, figures and technical report. It does not modify the original SEFI-PY engine or main research console equations.

`physical_reference.py` is an unchanged copy of `benchmarks/displacement_recovery/bosonic.py` from the prior local SEFI-PY research benchmark. Other new files describe and implement the additions listed above. Attribution and the existing research license are retained. Commercial use remains subject to that license.

Computational checks performed by the assistant are documented in the validation records. Author review and an independent external reproduction have not been completed or asserted. No novel SEFI inference algorithm or general superiority claim is established by this addition.
