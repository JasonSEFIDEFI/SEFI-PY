## Distinctness and practical collaborators

Describe this as a companion computational robustness benchmark, not a replacement for a submitted foundational manuscript. Disclose the older displacement benchmark and related work. The estimators here use conventional Gaussian moment tracking and expert mixtures; novelty has not been established by a systematic literature review. Synthetic auxiliary probes are not a measured stabilizer syndrome. Experimental collaborators would need to establish common-mode noise, achievable calibration, detector/actuator effects and a real timing requirement before hardware deployment.
