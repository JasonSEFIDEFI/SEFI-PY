Baseline protocol: inspect array and wiring → verify drive waveform with a scope → test at reduced output → introduce a suitable particle → establish static hold → calibrate position tracking → repeat.

Conventional baseline SIMULATED

Command trajectory → speed limiter → geometric phase compiler → illustrative acoustic field. Synthetic particle dynamics use a damped second-order PD response. The field and particle response are separate models.

a = Kp · R · (command − position) − (Kd + damping) · velocity

R is an arbitrary amplitude/diameter/density/geometry response factor. Gains have toy-model units. No Gor’kov potential, radiation force, gravity balance, scattering, thermal model, calibrated pressure or trap stability analysis is implemented. Frequency and phase alter the field visualization, not synthetic dynamics.

Commanded means the speed-limited command sent to the simulated controller; configured trajectory may briefly lead it. Command speed limit does not certify physical particle speed.

Evidence vocabulary

Safety panel

SIM E-STOP pauses simulation and zeros simulated velocity. It cannot disconnect power or stop physical hardware.

Before bench work, establish a physical power cutoff, appropriate driver protection, enclosure and particle containment, electrical/thermal checks, and an exposure assessment for ultrasound and audible subharmonics. Do not infer safe sound pressure from normalized amplitude.

Current configured boundaries are simulation guardrails, not certified hardware limits. Out-of-range settings prevent Run. No laser is modeled; use appropriate illumination controls if one is later introduced.
